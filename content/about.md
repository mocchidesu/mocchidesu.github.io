I started out as a network and VoIP engineer, spent several years at startups, and then moved into platform engineering. Today I work on service mesh infrastructure (Istio/Envoy) at one of the world's largest e-commerce companies, covering more than 600 production Kubernetes clusters, and well over 1,000 in total, across North America, Asia and Europe.

Most of what I write here is root-cause analysis from that work: things that only break at scale, and what they taught me about the layers underneath.

Opinions are my own. Incident details are anonymized.
